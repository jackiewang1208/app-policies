01 · Who we are

This policy describes the current offline Android version of Arrow Trails (also called 箭径), developed and provided by Jackie Liao (“we”, “us”). It also explains what happens when you contact us or visit this policy website.

02 · Data on your device

The game stores the following in its local app storage:

  • Completed levels and best star ratings, used to track progress and unlock levels.
  • Your unfinished puzzle state, including moves, mistakes, hints used and any remaining timer, so you can resume a game.
  • Language and haptic-feedback preferences, used to remember your settings.

These records are used for gameplay on your device. We do not receive them, and the game has no developer-operated cloud save, account system or cross-device sync.

03 · Collection & sharing

The game does not collect or transmit personal information to us. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. It does not collect advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos or payment details.

We do not sell personal information or share gameplay records with advertisers or data brokers. Information you voluntarily send by email is handled separately as described in section 06.

04 · Permissions & security

Playing does not require access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or shared photos and files. Local saves use the app's private storage and the access protections provided by Android. The game is configured to exclude its saved data from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

We cannot access or remotely recover your local save. Your device's security and access controls help protect it; no storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

05 · Retention & deletion

Local progress and preferences remain until replaced during play or removed. To erase them, open Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Trails → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data. Menu names vary by device. Uninstalling the app normally also removes its local data.

Deleting this data resets your progress and settings. We hold no server copy to restore. There is no game account to delete. Any copies created independently through your device tools must be managed through those tools.

06 · Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond, investigate your issue and handle privacy requests. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Our email provider processes this correspondence to deliver and store email. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to address your request and any applicable legal obligations, and then delete it. You may request access, correction or deletion by contacting the address below, subject to applicable law. We do not use support emails for advertising.

07 · Website & other services

This policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or cookies to these pages. When you visit, GitHub may process technical request information such as your IP address to deliver and protect its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your operating system, app store and email provider may independently process information under their own policies and your settings. Those services are distinct from the game's local save data.

08 · Children's privacy

The game's no-account, no-tracking approach applies to all players. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the game. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information to us by email, please contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

09 · Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app or our data practices change. The updated policy will appear at this address with a revised date. If changes require additional notice or consent, we will provide it as required. This policy describes the current offline game, not features that might be introduced in the future.
